Keyboard Shortcuts

Guided Tasks

  1. Task 1: In "Moving Magnet" mode, vary the magnet speed from 0.2 to 2.0 m/s and observe how induced EMF changes proportionally.
  2. Task 2: In "Generator" mode, change coil turns from 50 to 500 and verify that peak EMF increases proportionally (EMF ∝ N).
  3. Task 3: In "Transformer" mode, keep primary voltage at 230V, change secondary turns, and verify Vs/Vp ≈ Ns/Np ratio.

Physics Implementation
